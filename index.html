Privacy Policy: ውዳሴ ማርያም (Widase Mariam)

Weegix Technology Solutions LLC · Effective September 30, 2026

This policy describes how the ውዳሴ ማርያም (Widase Mariam) app for Android handles information. In short: the app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account or sign-in, and it does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, or usage analytics.

Information kept on your device

The app saves a few reading preferences only on your own phone, so it can remember them the next time you open it:

  • text size and night mode,
  • bookmarks and the last section you read.

These never leave your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes them.

Internet, ads and third parties

All prayers, hymns and the calendar are built into the app and work offline. The app shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking tools, and does not share data with third parties.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Content sources

Prayer texts are from the Widase Mariam prayer book of the Eritrean Orthodox Tewahdo Church. Hymns are used with permission from ፈለገ ሰላም ቤ/ት ሰንበት ደብረ ሣህል ቅዱስ ሚካኤል ወኣቡነ ኣረጋዊ, London (Debre Sahl St Michael Eritrean Orthodox Tewahdo Church, Sunday School, London UK).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
